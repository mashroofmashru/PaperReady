## 💡 The Problem

Visiting a government department, passport seva kendra, transport authority, or civic centre often turns into a frustrating ordeal:
- **Missing documents:** Arriving at the counter only to discover you needed an original copy, a specific gazetted attestation, or a utility bill issued within the last 90 days.
- **Fragmented portals:** Official government websites are frequently split across multiple departments, loaded with outdated circulars, or buried under confusing bureaucratic jargon.
- **Wasted time:** Multiple trips, missed appointments, and delayed applications simply because the document requirements were not crystal clear beforehand.

---

## 🎯 What We Are Building

**PaperReady** is an open-source, community-driven platform designed to eliminate the guesswork before your appointments.

Instead of navigating complex government notices, citizens get a clean, interactive, step-by-step preparation dashboard.

### Key Features (Planned)

- 📑 **Interactive Checklists**: Check off original documents, self-attested photocopies, and digital files as you assemble your folder.
- 🔀 **Conditional Requirements**: Tailored requirements based on your specific situation (e.g., *Fresh Application* vs *Renewal*, *Adult* vs *Minor*, *Tatkaal/Expedited* vs *Normal*).
- 📐 **Format & Spec Cheat-Sheets**: Exact photo dimensions, acceptable background colours, signature dimensions, file size limits, and validity rules.
- 🖨️ **Printable & Offline-Ready**: Export your checklist to PDF or print a clean one-page summary to clip to your physical file folder.
- 👥 **Crowdsourced & Community-Verified**: Real citizen reports confirming what officers actually ask for on the ground and flagging recent policy changes.
- ⚡ **Lightweight & Privacy-First**: No signup required to view checklists; your checklist progress is stored locally on your device.

| Category | Example Services Covered |
| :--- | :--- |
| **🛂 Passports & Travel** | Fresh Passport, Passport Renewal, Minor Passport, Name Change, Lost/Damaged Replacement |

## 🗺️ Product Roadmap

- [ ] **Phase 1: Foundation & Curation**
  - Project architecture & modern, accessible web interface setup
  - Structured schema definition for document requirements (JSON/YAML)
  - Curate high-demand application checklists (Passport & Driving Licence)
- [ ] **Phase 2: Dynamic Questionnaires**
  - Smart questionnaire to filter checklists based on user criteria (age, state/region, application type)
  - Search and filter by department or document type
- [ ] **Phase 3: Utility & Offline Features**
  - Local storage persistence for checklist progress
  - One-click PDF checklist export & printer-friendly layout
- [ ] **Phase 4: Community Contribution Pipeline**
  - Guide contribution templates for contributors to add local city/state procedures
  - Verification workflow for regulatory updates
